Privacy Policy

Trustee Monitor · Pine Time Properties · Effective September 28, 2026

Trustee Monitor is a private, internal tool. It is used only by the owner of Pine Time Properties, on the owner's own computer.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses two Google permissions on the owner's own Google account:

  • Google Calendar: to create and update events for upcoming trustee sales on a calendar the tool creates for this purpose.
  • Gmail (send only): to send update emails to the owner. The tool cannot read, search or delete any email.

How the data is handled

  • The tool does not collect data from anyone other than the owner.
  • Google data is not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
